How it works

From invitation to table in four steps.

  1. ChooseFind the invitations you'd like to experience, tick as many as you want, and hit request. Add dates if you have them in mind — if not, we'll suggest some.
  2. We confirmWe'll check with the venue and email you to confirm your date and time, or to offer you some. Your visit isn't booked until you receive our confirmation email.
  3. EnjoyTurn up with your plus one and enjoy everything included in your invitation — it's on us. If you order anything outside the invitation, you'll just pay for that separately.
  4. ShareCreate the content listed on your invitation and share it within 7 days, tagging the venue and @thewonderagency. We'll include all the handles and details in your confirmation.
Good to know
Is everything complimentary?
Yes. Everything listed under Your Invite is covered for you and your plus one. Anything additional you order is yours to pay for.
Can I request more than one invitation?
Absolutely — tick as many as you like on the form. Each one is confirmed separately, and because places are limited we can't guarantee every request.
Is my visit booked when I submit the form?
Not yet. We check every request with the venue and email you, either confirming your date or offering some. Please wait for our confirmation before making plans or contacting the venue.
What if I need to cancel?
Just reply to your confirmation email as soon as you can — ideally at least 48 hours before your visit — so we can offer the space to someone else.
Who should I contact?
Once confirmed, we'll send you everything you need for your visit. For anything else, email hello@thewonder.group.
