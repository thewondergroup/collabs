What we ask for

Four deliverables, every visit.

The same for every invitation unless it says otherwise. Please share everything within 7 days of your visit and send Ciara the links.

  1. A reel or a carouselOne or the other — your call. Tell us which when you request. No copyrighted music on the reel (see 4).
  2. Stories on the dayA few stories while you're there, tagging the venue.
  3. Add the venue as a collaboratorOn the reel or carousel, use Instagram's Invite collaborator so it appears on the venue's profile too. We'll send the handle with your confirmation.
  4. Share a partnership ad codeWe'd love to put a little spend behind your content so more people see it. To do that we need the ad code from your reel or carousel — and Instagram won't allow boosted content with copyrighted music, so please use original or royalty-free audio.
    How to get the ad code

    On a post you've already shared: open it from your profile → tap the three dots (top right) → Partnership label & ads → turn on Get partnership ad code → tap Copy, or Share to DM it to us.

    While creating: before you post, tap Partnership label & ads (for stories, the label icon at the top) → turn on Get partnership ad code → share as normal. Once it's live, copy the code or share it with us.

    You can turn the toggle off at any time to stop new ads being made; anything already running carries on.

Good to know
Is everything complimentary?
Yes. Everything listed under Your Invite is covered for you and your plus one. Anything additional you order is yours to pay for.
Why do you need an ad code?
So we can put a small budget behind your post and get it in front of more people — it runs from the venue's account with your name on it, and you keep full control of the toggle. It just means no copyrighted music on the reel.
Can I request more than one invitation?
Absolutely — tick as many as you like on the form. Each one is confirmed separately, and because places are limited we can't guarantee every request.
Is my visit booked when I submit the form?
Not yet. We check your date and time with the venue and email you to confirm, or offer an alternative if it's full. Please wait for our confirmation before making plans or contacting the venue.
What if I need to cancel?
Use your bookings page — the link is in every email we send you. You can move or cancel any booking there in a couple of taps, and the venue is told straight away. Please give at least 48 hours where you can, so we can offer the space to someone else.
How do I hear about the next ones?
Follow @the.wonder.list on Instagram — new invitations go up there first.
Who should I contact?
Once confirmed, we'll send you everything you need for your visit. For anything else, email hello@thewonder.group.
